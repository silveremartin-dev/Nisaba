📖 Nisaba — Epigraphic User Manual & Quick Guide

What is Nisaba?

Nisaba is a high-throughput epigraphic computational suite for ancient Sumerian cuneiform. It turns raw archival text into certified columnar databases, dates broken tablets, attributes scribes, audits double-entry accounting ledgers, and detects cross-museum virtual joins.

1. Cross-Linking & Navigation

Everything in the tablet dossier is interconnected: click any official name in the transliteration or transaction table to filter all tablets involving that official. Click a scribe name or Chronos date to find related chancery tablets. Click a candidate join fragment ID to open that tablet immediately.

2. Category Filter Chips

Use the chips in the sidebar to jump between: ⭐ Showcase (tablets with photos and verified joins), ✍️ Scribes (35,000+ tablets with attributed scribes), ⏳ Chronos (dated tablets), 📊 Ledger (accounting transactions), and 🧩 Joins (cross-museum fragment joins).

3. Metrological Measures & Metric Equivalents

Nisaba parses ancient Sumerian capacity (gur, barig, ban2, sila3) and weight units (gu2, ma-na, gin2), providing modern metric equivalents (liters of grain/beer, kilograms of wool/metals, discrete livestock counts).

4. Prosopographic Interaction Graph

Click the 🕸️ Prosopography Graph link in the top bar to inspect the interactive social and bureaucratic network of hundreds of Sumerian officials, with direct clickable links back to their tablets.
